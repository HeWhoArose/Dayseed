### A. Morning Daypart: "Sage Modern" (Theme B in Reference)
- **Mood & Atmosphere:** Clean, contemporary, and calm. Gentle morning light, spacious, airy, and fresh.
- **Palette Swatches:**
  1. Soft Sage (muted desaturated green)
  2. Cream (bright warm off-white canvas)
  3. Stone (subtle neutral grey-beige for borders and secondary text)
  4. Leaf Green (vibrant organic primary green)
- **Canvas / Background:** Crisp, light cream-white (`#F9F8F5`).
- **Primary Action Button ("+ Log today"):** Muted leaf/sage green pill button.
- **Header Illustration / Picture:**
  - **Subject:** Fresh organic botanical leafy branch.
  - **Style:** Clean, modern vector/watercolor leaf sprig positioned in the top-right header area.
  - **Colour:** Soft sage and olive green tones against the airy light canvas.
- **Card Surfaces:** Pure white or very soft stone containers with subtle, low-contrast borders.

---

### B. Midday / Daylight Daypart: "Field Journal" (Theme A in Reference)
- **Mood & Atmosphere:** Warm, editorial, botanical, and personal. Tactile paper warmth with refined hierarchy.
- **Palette Swatches:**
  1. Warm Cream (parchment/paper background)
  2. Sage (soft muted green for cards and accents)
  3. Olive (deep organic green for text and primary elements)
  4. Clay (warm earthy terracotta accent)
- **Canvas / Background:** Rich warm cream / parchment tone (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Primary Action Button ("+ Log today"):** Deep forest/olive green pill button.
- **Header Illustration / Picture:**
  - **Subject:** Detailed botanical branch with delicate leaves.
  - **Style:** Soft watercolor/pencil botanical illustration in the top-right header, evoking a naturalist's notebook.
  - **Colour:** Olive, sage, and subtle sepia/clay undertones.
- **Card Surfaces:** Cream and light sage tinted cards (`#EAEFE6`) with gentle borders.

---

### C. Evening / Dusk Daypart: "Earth & Clay" (Theme C in Reference)
- **Mood & Atmosphere:** Richer, warmer, and grounded. Sunset warmth, tactile and peaceful as the day winds down.
- **Palette Swatches:**
  1. Clay / Terracotta (warm burnt sienna / rich terracotta)
  2. Sage (muted earthy green secondary accent)
  3. Warm Stone (warm sand/beige card backgrounds)
  4. Olive (deep earth for grounding text and borders)
- **Canvas / Background:** Warm peachy/terracotta-tinted stone cream (`#F6EFE6`).
- **Primary Action Button ("+ Log today"):** **Rich Terracotta / Clay** pill button (distinct from the green used in morning/midday).
- **Header Illustration / Picture:**
  - **Subject:** Rolling hills/horizon landscape under a sunset sky.
  - **Style:** Layered atmospheric hills in terracotta, clay, peach, and muted sage with a gentle setting sun.
  - **Colour:** Warm terracotta earth, peach sunset gradient, and dusky sage shadows.
- **Card Surfaces:** Warm stone/peach-tinted containers (`#EFE6DC`) with terracotta and olive accents.

---

### D. Alternative Fixed Light: "Minimal Calm" (Theme D in Reference)
- **Mood & Atmosphere:** Clean, minimal, and refined. High clarity, reduced decorative styling, peaceful utility.
- **Palette Swatches:**
  1. Light Cream (clean subtle warm-white)
  2. Sage (restrained green accent)
  3. Neutral Grey (calm, legible slate)
  4. Soft Green (pale tint for quiet highlights)
- **Canvas / Background:** Light neutral off-white (`#FAF9F6`).
- **Primary Action Button ("+ Log today"):** Deep dark sage green pill button.
- **Header Illustration / Picture:**
  - **Subject:** Minimal single-stem botanical leaves.
  - **Style:** Understated, delicate watercolor sprig with ample breathing room.
  - **Colour:** Pale sage and neutral slate.

---

### E. Night Daypart / Dark Mode: "Night Garden" (Theme E in Reference)
- **Mood & Atmosphere:** Calm and elegant at night. Dark mode with reduced contrast, soothing warmth, and zero harsh glare.
- **Palette Swatches:**
  1. Deep Green (rich midnight forest / pine charcoal, not pure black)
  2. Warm Neutral (soft parchment white for high-legibility text)
  3. Sage (luminous soft green for accents)
  4. Clay (muted warm amber/terracotta for subtle warmth)
- **Canvas / Background:** Deep nocturnal pine green (`#121D17` / `#16221B`).
- **Primary Action Button ("+ Log today"):** Luminous soft sage green pill with deep pine text/icon.
- **Header Illustration / Picture:**
  - **Subject:** Glowing crescent moon nestled among night garden botanical foliage.
  - **Style:** Soft luminous golden crescent moon with warm diffuse glow, framed by dark silhouette botanical leaves and stars/stippling.
  - **Colour:** Golden amber moon glow (`#E8C882`) paired with deep forest foliage.
- **Card Surfaces:** Dark elevated pine containers (`#1D2B22`) with subtle borders and soft sage text.

## 3. Shared Component & Design Language Across All Themes

The bottom reference panel specifies the shared visual foundation:

1. **Navigation Bar:**
   - Same 4-destination structure: Today (home), History (calendar/journal), Insights (graph), Settings (gear).
   - Filled/highlighted active icon in the theme's primary accent (green or clay); outline for inactive.
2. **Log Today Action Button:**
   - Full-width or centered pill button with `+` icon and bold legible label.
   - Dynamic theme color: Green for Morning/Day/Minimal, **Terracotta/Clay for Evening**, Luminous Sage for Night.
3. **Focus / Habit List Items:**
   - Open circular state indicator (`○`), rounded card boundaries, clean typography.
4. **Journal Cards:**
   - Rounded card with photo thumbnail or botanical sprig icon, clear title, and subtle timestamp.
5. **Growth Cards:**
   - Compact summary badge with subtle metric icon and non-intrusive progress.
6. **Consistent Illustration Style:**
   - Subtle, organic, and hand-crafted: botanical sprigs, soft watercolor leaves, and layered terracotta hills. Never clip-art, generic 3D, or fluorescent gradients.

## 4. Comprehensive Audit: Current Code vs. Reference Design

An audit of `app/src/main/java/com/example/dayseed/ui/theme/` and `TodayScreen.kt` reveals the following findings:

### 4.1 Matches with Reference
- ✅ **Base Botanical Aesthetic:** The core brand identity in `Color.kt` and `DESIGN.md` uses sage and dark pine tones (`LightBrand = #286143`, `DarkCanvas = #121A15`), which align well with the overall botanical philosophy.
- ✅ **Daypart Architecture:** `SoftBotanicalThemeConfig` in `Color.kt` already defines 4 time-of-day slots:
  - Morning (05:00 - 10:59)
  - Day (11:00 - 16:59)
  - Evening (17:00 - 20:59)
  - Night (21:00 - 04:59)
- ✅ **Dark Theme Base:** `DarkCanvas` (`#121A15`) and `DarkSurface` (`#1D2820`) capture the deep pine tone of the "Night Garden".

## 5. Implementation Roadmap to Match the Design

To bring the current codebase into 100% compliance with the owner's design:

1. **Update `Color.kt`:**
   - Define exact swatches for the 5 themes:
     - `MorningSage`, `MorningLeafGreen`, `MorningStone`
     - `EveningClay` (rich terracotta), `EveningSunsetPeach`, `EveningWarmStone`
     - `NightPineDeep`, `NightMoonGlow` (golden crescent amber)
2. **Update `Theme.kt`:**
   - Ensure `EveningColorScheme` sets `primary = EveningClay` so buttons and active highlights turn terracotta during evening hours.
3. **Create Daypart Header Illustrations:**
   - Add composable/vector illustrations or assets representing:
     - Morning leafy branch
     - Midday field botanicals
     - Evening sunset rolling hills
     - Night crescent moon with foliage
4. **Integrate Header into `TodayScreen.kt`:**
   - Render the appropriate daypart illustration in the top-right of the Today screen header beside the date and greeting.
