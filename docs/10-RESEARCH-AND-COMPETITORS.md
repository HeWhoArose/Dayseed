# Research and Competitors

**Status:** Research plan, not a completed market study. No competitor claims in this document should be treated as verified findings.

## 1. Research questions
- How do current habit apps handle missed days, flexible goals, rest days, and restarting?
- Which apps support short daily journaling alongside habit tracking?
- What does “offline support” mean in practice: launch, create/edit, history, export, reminders?
- Which apps require accounts, sync, subscriptions, or ads?
- What privacy and deletion controls are clearly explained?
- Which accessibility and small-screen patterns work well?
- Is the proposed effort-level model understandable without tutorial-heavy onboarding?

## 2. Competitor set to investigate
Select a balanced sample across habit trackers, journaling apps, and combined wellbeing tools. Possible starting points include Loop Habit Tracker, HabitNow, TickTick habits, Daylio, Finch, and comparable current products. This is a research shortlist only, not an endorsement or claim about present features, pricing, privacy, or availability.

## 3. Evidence protocol
For each product, record:
- Product name, platform, version/build date, region tested, and date of review.
- Official product/store page and official privacy policy.
- Pricing and free-tier limitations as observed at the time.
- Whether key flows work in airplane mode, tested directly where possible.
- How missed days and edits are represented, with screenshots only where permitted.
- Backup, export, deletion, account, analytics, and ad disclosures.
- Accessibility observations from actual testing.
- Findings separated into **observed**, **vendor-claimed**, and **unknown**.

Do not infer implementation details from marketing copy. Do not claim a feature is absent unless it was checked in a sufficiently current version and region.

## 4. User research plan
Conduct short interviews or moderated task tests with people who have tried habit trackers and people who have abandoned them. Avoid leading questions. Ask participants to demonstrate how they record a small action, handle a missed day, and resume after a break. Ask what language feels supportive versus patronising. Obtain consent and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive health details.

## 5. Competitive differentiation hypothesis
Dayseed may differentiate through effort-aware completion, history-preserving corrections, local-first privacy, lightweight journaling, and a deliberately nonjudgmental comeback experience. Treat this as a hypothesis until supported by competitor review and user feedback.

## 6. Research log template
For each source or session record:
- Date accessed/session date
- Source URL or participant code
- Question investigated
- Method
- Evidence/observation
- Confidence and limitations
- Implication for Dayseed

## 7. Research quality gate
Every external factual claim added elsewhere must have a source that was actually consulted and a date where freshness matters. If research is incomplete, say so plainly. Update the decision log only when evidence supports a decision.
