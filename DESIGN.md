# Dayseed Design Overview

**Status:** Exploration only; not approved.

Dayseed's design should express its promise: **Your progress is more than your streak.** It should be encouraging without infantilising users, calm without becoming generic, and practical on small Android screens.

## Directions under consideration
- **Field Journal:** warm, tactile, reflective.
- **Living Editorial:** confident typography, strong hierarchy, restrained growth motif.
- **Quiet Instrument:** minimal, precise, utility-led.

The current recommendation is to test a synthesis of warmth, editorial clarity, and strong usability. This is not an approved visual system.

## Required design outputs
See [UX and Design](docs/02-UX-AND-DESIGN.md) and the [Design Review Checklist](docs/DESIGN-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md). The design gate requires wireframes, primary user flows, component and token rules, accessibility checks, copy review, and explicit approval.

Do not create app screens or a website as a substitute for completing and reviewing the design plan.
